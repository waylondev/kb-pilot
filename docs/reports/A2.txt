# D3. What is UPI and how does ZX Bank Asia support it?

**Question:** What is UPI and how does ZX Bank Asia support it?

**Answer:** UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is a real-time payment system that enables instant money transfers between bank accounts using a mobile device. It works 24x7, lets a single app access multiple bank accounts, and enables easy transactions via mobile number, Virtual Payment Address (VPA), or QR codes for fund transfers, bill payments, and merchant payments.

ZX Bank Asia supports UPI through its mobile app:
- **Activation:** Download the ZX Bank Asia app (Android/iOS), log in or register with the registered mobile number (complete e-KYC if needed), tap the UPI section, create or link a UPI ID (VPA), link your bank account, and set/reset a 4/6-digit UPI PIN authenticated with debit card details.
- **Limits:** ₹1,00,000 per transaction and per day (total across all UPI transactions), with a maximum of 20 transfers per day, subject to NPCI and ZX Bank policies.
- **Support:** In-app Help & Support, toll-free customer care (1800-XXX-XXXX), email upi.support@zxbank.asia, and the AI assistant "Zia".
- **Availability:** UPI is currently available in India for ZX Bank account holders; cross-border payments use dedicated remittance options instead.

# P37. How safe is ZX NetBanking transaction protection?

**Question:** How safe is a ZX NetBanking transaction protection?
**Answer:** ZX Bank's NetBanking transactions are protected through a layered security framework and enforced by the NetBanking security best practices.

1. **Multi-factor authentication (MFA):** account access and transactions require biometric login (fingerprint/facial recognition on the app) and a One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your registered mobile/email; new device logins trigger additional verification steps (Safety Features, L5-9).
2. **AI-driven fraud detection:** AI systems monitor account activity in real-time, flag suspicious behavior, and send immediate fraud alerts for any unauthorized or suspicious transaction attempts (L10-12).
3. **End-to-end encryption:** all communications and financial transactions are secured with industry-standard 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption; sensitive customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest (L14-16).
4. **Secure transfer controls:** daily/per-transaction limits apply, especially for new payees or high-risk transactions, and adding new beneficiaries requires additional authentication (L22-24).
5. **Transaction notifications:** every transaction and any change to account settings triggers instant SMS, email, and push notifications (L34-35).
6. **Personal practice:** ZX NetBanking users should never share their password/OTP, update passwords regularly, login only via the official website or ZX Bank Asia app, and enable two-factor authentication (NetBanking Guide, L56-60).
