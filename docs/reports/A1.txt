# A5. Why might ZX Bank Asia impose daily transaction limits on UPI?

**Question:** Why might ZX Bank Asia impose daily transaction limits on UPI?

**Answer:** ZX Bank Asia's UPI daily limits — ₹1,00,000 per transaction and per day, with a maximum of 20 transfers per day — act as **risk and compliance controls**. The document notes that limits follow **NPCI and ZX Bank policies** and that the maximum number of transfers "may vary based on customer risk profile and regulatory updates". In other words, capping the exposure per transaction/per day contains potential losses from fraud or error, tailors risk limits to each customer's profile, and keeps the bank in line with NPCI and regulatory requirements (e.g., the money-laundering/privacy requirement). The cap also directly supports the bank's broader secure-transfer controls.

# A12. Why does ZX Bank Ltd. offer personal loans without collateral?

**Question:** Why does ZX Bank Ltd. offer personal loans without collateral?

**Answer:** ZX Bank structures its personal loans to be collateral-free — "Zero Collateral: no need for security or guarantor" — as a deliberate product feature. Unsecured personal loans make borrowing accessible to salaried applicants who may not have security to pledge; they rely instead on credit assessment (income, salary slips, bank statements) rather than assets. This keeps the product fast and convenient: quick approval, instant digital eligibility check, and flexible 12–60 month tenors. The trade-off shown is higher cost for unsecured risk — rates for lower-credit markets (e.g., Bangladesh/Bhutan/Nepal 13.50%–21.00%) are higher — which protects the bank while customers avoid pledging collateral.

# A14. Why is it important to act quickly when reporting a fraud transaction at ZX Bank?

**Question:** Why is it important to act quickly when reporting a fraud transaction at ZX Bank?

**Answer:** Acting fast protects your money and maximizes the chance of resolution. ZX Bank's guide opens with "If you suspect or identify a fraudulent transaction on your ZX Bank account, follow this guide immediately to secure your funds and resolve the issue," and it closes "Report suspicious activities as soon as possible to minimize risk." The practical reasons:
- **Stop the bleeding:** As soon as you report, you can block the card or freeze the account (Step 2 of the process) so no further unauthorized charges can occur.
- **Protect evidence:** When you act quickly the key details — date/time, amount, SMS/email alerts, screenshots — are fresh, which supports an accurate written complaint (Step 4).
- **Faster resolution tracking:** you receive a reference number and ZX targets resolution within 10 working days; quick reporting starts that clock earlier, helping you get your funds recovered faster.

# C22. How ZX Fixed Deposit differs from regular savings account?

**Question:** How ZX Fixed Deposit differs from regular savings account?

**Answer:** A ZX Fixed Deposit (FD) is a locked-in deposit product while the Savings Account is a flexible, day-to-day transactional account. The FD deliberately "offers higher returns compared to regular savings accounts" by paying fixed interest for a chosen tenure ranging from 7 days to 10 years — sample rates run from 3.50% (7–45 days) up to 6.30% (3–5 years), with a higher senior-citizen slab — and a minimum deposit from ₹5,000 (India). It additionally allows a loan/overdraft of up to 90% of the FD value and premature withdrawal, though closure charges may apply. By contrast, the regular savings account earns a lower rate on the daily balance and carries a minimum balance of ₹5,000 in India (regular variant) or nil for the digital zero-balance variant, and is used for free international debit card, UPI/QR payments, reward points and daily banking. In short, the FD trades liquidity for higher locked-in returns; the savings account trades guaranteed returns for daily transactional flexibility.

# C28. How NetBanking self-registration differs from branch activation methods?

**Question:** How NetBanking self-registration differs from branch activation methods?

**Answer:** The two methods differ mainly in where the steps happen and how credentials are issued. Self-registration (online) is done entirely by the customer on the ZX Bank website: they open the NetBanking Login page, select "New User/Register", enter their Customer ID (from passbook/statement) and registered mobile number, verify with an OTP, then set their username, password, transactional password and security questions themselves — no branch visit is required. Branch activation, in contrast, requires physically visiting any ZX Bank branch, filling in a NetBanking registration form, and then receiving a welcome kit or activation link on mobile/email from the bank, after which the customer sets the login and transaction password. So online self-registration is self-serve and immediate with OTP verification, while branch activation is bank-mediated, form-based and hands the customer the activation link/welcome kit before they can set passwords. A third path — activation via the ZX Bank Asia mobile app — follows the online self-registration steps inside the app.
